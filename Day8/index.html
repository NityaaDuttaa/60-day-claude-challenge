      📝 Executive Summary

      senior analyst read-out

      Data sources & methodology: Air quality — IQAir (iqair.com), aqi.in and aqicn.org (CPCB-fed monitoring stations), captured Aug 6, 2026, US AQI standard. PM2.5/PM10 concentrations shown are direct station readings where available (Delhi, Faridabad) and otherwise estimated from the reported Overall AQI using US EPA breakpoint conversion tables — treat estimated values as indicative, not laboratory-grade. Water-quality scores are composite estimates built from CPCB National Water Quality Monitoring Programme river-stretch data, Bureau of Indian Standards tap-water testing (Mumbai ranked purest / Delhi worst of 21 cities tested), and CPCB state-level water quality scores — India has no single unified real-time per-city water index, so these figures are directional, not live sensor data. This tool is for general awareness and does not replace medical, dermatological, or public-health advice.

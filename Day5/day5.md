## Observations

- Prompt A generated a more generic response because it lacked personal context.
- Prompt B produced a much more personalized and relevant response.
- Providing background information helped Claude understand the objective better.
- The recommendations became more practical and tailored to my goals.
- Context significantly improved the quality, accuracy, and usefulness of the response.

---

## Key Learnings

- Context is one of the most important parts of prompt engineering.
- The more relevant information you provide, the better the AI can assist.
- Personalized prompts lead to responses that are more actionable and specific.
- Small changes in prompt context can greatly influence output quality.
